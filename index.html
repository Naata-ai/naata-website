Features
What naata does
naata क्या करता है
🤫
It knows when to stay quiet
Most AI wants the last word. naata doesn't. In the Bridge it says nothing at all while the two of you are talking — it speaks when you call it by name, or when the same argument has clearly been going in circles. The room stays yours.
🎙️
Speak, don't type
Say it out loud and naata transcribes it — and it answers in a Hindi voice when you're speaking Hindi. Useful at the end of a long day when a keyboard is the last thing you want.
💬
Hindi, Hinglish, English
Switch mid-sentence like everyone actually does. "Mujhe bahut bura laga" lands with the same weight as its English equivalent — not translated, understood.
🎭
Reads tone, not just words
It notices when a two-word reply is really frustration, and when a calm sentence is carrying something heavier underneath.
🔁
Spots the loop you're stuck in
The same argument in different clothes, every few weeks. naata tracks patterns across your conversations and names them — the thing that's hard to see from inside the fight.
🧘
Something to actually try
Appreciation Rounds, Active Listening, Softened Startup — short exercises drawn from established relationship research, offered when they fit rather than as homework.
📋
A weekly read on how you're doing
Daily mood check-ins and emotion tracking add up to a weekly summary — what came up, what shifted, what's worth attention.
👨‍👩‍👧
Not only for couples
A parent and a grown child, two siblings, close friends. naata adapts its tone to the bond you pick — it never assumes romance where there isn't any.
🛟
It knows when to step back
If something said points to real danger, naata stops coaching and surfaces India's crisis helplines — Kiran, iCall, Tele-MANAS. It is not a substitute for professional help, and it says so.
Safety first: If you or your partner are experiencing a mental health crisis or domestic violence, please reach out to a helpline — Kiran (1800-599-0019), iCall (9152987821), or Tele-MANAS (14416). Your safety always comes first.
